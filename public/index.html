Nicki Broichhausen

Computer vision & robotics for space

I’m a master’s student in Satellite Technology and Advanced Space Systems at the University of Würzburg, with a bachelor’s in computer science (specialization in artificial intelligence). My master’s thesis at the German Aerospace Center (DLR), Institute of Robotics and Mechatronics, is on real-time visual odometry for micro aerial vehicles.

I work on computer vision and robot perception for space: robust stereo visual odometry for flight in planetary-exploration scenarios, learning-based attitude control, and onboard software for small satellites. During a semester abroad in a CubeSat laboratory in Tokyo, I worked on a satellite project with JAXA.
